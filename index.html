About Us

Language is about connection, not perfection.

Learning a new language should not feel like deciphering an ancient code. For most people, the hardest part of English is not a lack of trying, but the overwhelming sentence structures, confusing word origins, and the complete lack of practice with simple, everyday small talk.

We started Lingo English to bridge that gap and make English feel practical, welcoming, and useful in real life.

Why We Built This

Whether you are aiming to speak naturally in social settings or trying to elevate your academic work at school, we give you the tools to succeed in both your daily life and your future goals.

Breaking the Barrier

We watched thousands of learners hit the exact same wall: wanting to learn, but being priced out by the industry.

  • Tutors cost small fortunes.
  • Textbooks add up to hundreds of dollars.
  • Quality resources are buried behind high paywalls.

The people who need these tools the most often suffer the most. We believe that education should be universally accessible, regardless of your age, background, or income.

Lingo English exists to take away the financial burden so you can focus on what actually matters: finding your voice.

The Problem We’re Addressing

English is heavily commercialized.

1. The Cost of Learning English

  • Expensive tutors and apps: private language tutoring can cost $20 to $50+ per hour, and many popular apps lock essential features behind monthly subscriptions.
  • Cost of textbooks and tests: standardized exams such as TOEFL or IELTS and their preparation materials can cost $150 to $300+.
  • The resource divide: families with lower incomes often have to prioritize basic necessities over extracurricular learning materials, making paid courses and books out of reach.

2. The Global Access Gap

Lack of access is not just about money—it is also about infrastructure. Around 2.6 billion people remain offline, which means millions of eager learners cannot access free videos, articles, or online tools. In many rural or underfunded school districts, foreign language programs are either nonexistent, understaffed, or based on outdated memorization methods instead of practical communication.

3. Why This Matters for Lingo English

Language learning is one of the most powerful economic ladders in the world. Knowing English opens doors to global remote jobs, higher-paying careers, international university programs, scholarships, and access to a vast amount of educational content online. When language learning is trapped behind high paywalls, the people who need those opportunities the most are excluded.

What We Offer

We offer our services in a way that removes the pressure often attached to learning English. Instead of focusing only on grammar drills and expensive lessons, we build learning around confidence, practicality, and real communication.

  • Free of cost so learners can begin improving their English without worrying about tuition fees, subscriptions, or hidden charges.
  • Open to all ages because language learning should not be limited by age, school level, or life stage. We believe everyone deserves a chance to grow.
  • Volunteer support for learners ages 12–17 to create a safe, encouraging space where younger learners can practice speaking, ask questions, and build confidence with guidance.
  • Fluency first because being understood matters more than sounding perfect. We help learners speak naturally and comfortably, not robotically.
  • Practical learning based on real situations such as school, conversations, travel, work, and daily life. This makes the learning experience more useful and meaningful.
  • A supportive environment where mistakes are part of progress. We want learners to feel safe trying, improving, and expressing themselves without fear.

Our mission is simple: help every learner feel comfortable, capable, and confident when they speak. We believe communication should be welcoming, supportive, and meaningful, no matter your age, background, or experience level.

Whether you are a beginner taking your first steps or someone who has studied for years but still wants to speak more naturally, this space is designed to help you grow at your own pace.
