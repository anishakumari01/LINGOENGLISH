How English Connects Opportunity, Heart, and Soul

Why English Matters for Jobs

English is often the common language of international business, remote work, and higher education. For many employers, basic to intermediate English skills are a prerequisite for customer-facing roles, global teams, and technical positions where documentation and collaboration happen in English. Improving English fluency can directly increase access to higher-paying jobs, remote contract work, and career mobility across borders.

How Many People Are Affected?

English is spoken by roughly 1.5 billion people worldwide (including both native and second-language speakers). With the global population at about 8.3 billion, that means approximately 6.8 billion people are not English speakers. Many of those people may know a few words or phrases but are not proficient enough to understand or communicate comfortably in English. A reasonable estimate is that around 6.5–7 billion people worldwide do not have a functional understanding of English, meaning the majority of the world's population—over 80%—does not speak English fluently.

This gap disproportionately affects learners in regions with limited internet access, underfunded education systems, and low-income communities. When English is a gatekeeper for remote work, scholarships, or career advancement, those who cannot access or afford language learning fall further behind economically.

Real Effects

  • Lower access to international job listings and remote positions.
  • Reduced chances of acceptance into English-medium universities and exchange programs.
  • Limited ability to use the vast majority of online professional resources and tutorials.

English Connects the Heart and Soul

Beyond economics, language is how we connect. English can be a bridge between cultures, allowing people to share stories, music, and ideas across continents. The ability to speak with someone from another country opens empathy, creates friendships, and deepens understanding. For many learners, gaining English skills is not just a career move — it is a way to connect their heart and soul with people they would otherwise never meet.

Connecting the World Through Language: Heart, Mind, and Soul

Language is more than a tool for communication—it is a pathway to understanding, opportunity, and human connection. A nonprofit dedicated to helping people around the world learn English has the potential to transform lives by opening doors to education, employment, healthcare, technology, and cross-cultural relationships.

Connecting Through the Heart

When people can communicate with one another, fear and misunderstanding often give way to empathy and friendship. Learning English allows individuals from different countries and cultures to share their stories, traditions, dreams, and challenges. These conversations remind us that despite our differences, we share many of the same hopes: to care for our families, build meaningful lives, and contribute to our communities.

Connecting Through the Soul

The deepest impact of language is its ability to foster belonging. When someone can express themselves and feel understood, they gain confidence, dignity, and hope. Teaching English is not simply about words—it is about empowering people to share their voice while honoring their native language and culture. True connection comes from listening as much as speaking, creating relationships built on mutual respect rather than uniformity.

A Ripple Effect of Opportunity

Every person who learns English can create new opportunities not only for themselves but also for their families and communities. They may mentor others, access better education, build businesses, collaborate across borders, or advocate for important causes. The ripple effects extend across generations.

A nonprofit devoted to English education has the opportunity to become more than an educational organization—it can become a catalyst for global understanding. By teaching language with compassion, cultural respect, and a commitment to lifelong learning, it helps build a world where communication becomes connection, knowledge becomes opportunity, and understanding becomes peace.

When we connect through the heart, we cultivate compassion. When we connect through the mind, we inspire growth. When we connect through the soul, we recognize our shared humanity. Together, these connections can help create a more united, hopeful, and collaborative world.

What We Can Do

Removing financial and technical barriers to English learning widens economic and emotional horizons. Free, community-centered programs and practical, conversation-led lessons create immediate value — learners gain confidence, job readiness, and the chance to connect with others across cultures.
